The calculation is x = (L + v·τ) / 2, where L is the pipe length, v the wave speed and τ the arrival time at A minus the arrival time at B. Wave speeds are typical values. Use a measured speed for your pipe if you have one.

This tool is a design demonstration based on simulation. It has not been tested on real pipes, so confirm a location before any excavation.
